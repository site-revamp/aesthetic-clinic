# Aesthetic Clinic – Harley Street Medical Centre Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Aesthetic Clinic, operating under the Harley Street Medical Centre brand, is an Abu Dhabi-based medical aesthetics and cosmetic healthcare provider. Its services include dermatology, plastic surgery, cosmetic treatments and cosmetic gynaecology.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic's specialist-led services, improving treatment discovery and making consultation enquiries more accessible for prospective patients.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger First-Screen Positioning and Messaging

- Replaces the broad headline "Feel Beautiful, Be Beautiful" with clearer messaging about the clinic's medical and aesthetic services.
- Highlights specialist-led care and the clinic's Abu Dhabi location.
- Introduces a prominent consultation call to action.

**Issue addressed:** The original headline did not immediately communicate the clinic's main specialties or help visitors understand the services available.

### 2. Better Treatment Discovery and Organisation

- Organises treatments into six visual service categories.
- Separates dermatology, plastic surgery, cosmetic treatments and cosmetic gynaecology more clearly.
- Includes concise service descriptions and direct enquiry pathways.

**Issue addressed:** The original website lacked sufficiently intuitive treatment pathways, making it harder for visitors to find services related to concerns such as acne, pigmentation, skin rejuvenation and body contouring.

### 3. More Accessible Appointment and Contact Journey

- Improves the visibility of consultation buttons throughout the homepage.
- Makes telephone and enquiry actions easier to access, particularly on mobile.
- Gives clearer visibility to the clinic's location, opening hours and contact information.
- Creates a more direct route from treatment information to appointment enquiries.

**Issue addressed:** The original website did not make the next step from exploring treatments to contacting the clinic sufficiently prominent and straightforward.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Establishes a more professional medical aesthetics identity aligned with the Harley Street Medical Centre brand.
- **Layout and Hierarchy:** Creates a clearer first-screen introduction and separates the six treatment categories into structured sections.
- **User Experience:** Makes treatment discovery and consultation actions easier to follow.
- **Navigation:** Provides clearer pathways between treatment information and enquiry options.
- **Mobile Accessibility:** Prioritises visible contact and consultation actions for mobile visitors.
- **Content Consistency:** Improves the presentation of specialties, clinic information and patient-facing calls to action.
- **Overall Appearance:** Balances the clinic's premium aesthetic positioning with clear medical communication and a more conversion-focused homepage.

---

**Project Focus:** Stronger clinic positioning, structured treatment discovery and improved consultation enquiries.
